# ActivityWatch - Open Source Time Tracking for Focused Work

> **Understand your day: automatic tracking, clear reports, and privacy-first data ownership on every device you use.**

## About ActivityWatch

![ActivityWatch](https://encrypted-tbn0.gstatic.com/images?q=tbn:ANd9GcRfjEoKrPG9C542lQyGO5NwL7mt_NionxvJZyBpeKZ5mw&s)

ActivityWatch is an open source time tracking tool that helps you understand how your day is actually spent. Instead of manual timers and guesswork, ActivityWatch automatic tracking runs quietly in the background and records which applications and windows you use, then turns that raw data into readable reports. ActivityWatch time tracker is built for people who want honest feedback about their habits, not another dashboard full of vanity metrics.

The project is designed around ownership. Your data stays on your own machine, which means ActivityWatch privacy focused tracking is not an afterthought but the foundation. Whether you are a student trying to protect deep work blocks, a freelancer billing by project, or a team lead curious about meeting load, ActivityWatch productivity insights give you a calm, factual view of where attention goes.

ActivityWatch cross-platform support means the same workflow follows you between operating systems. You can start tracking on one device, review reports on another, and keep a consistent picture of your time without changing tools or retraining yourself. The interface favors clarity over decoration: timelines, categories, and summaries that answer simple questions like "what did I actually work on this week?"

Because ActivityWatch is open source, the community can inspect, extend, and adapt it. That transparency matters when the subject is your personal activity data. ActivityWatch time tracker stays useful over long periods because it is boring in the best way: it starts with your session, records reliably, and gives you a report you can trust for planning, reflection, and better boundaries.

## Why This Exists

Most time tracking forces a choice between manual timers that you forget to start and invasive tools that send your activity to someone else's server. **ActivityWatch** solves this fragmentation by putting automatic measurement, local storage, and readable reporting into one calm environment.

ActivityWatch solves **one specific problem**: uncertain awareness of where working hours actually go. Just open ActivityWatch, choose a period, and see your activity with timelines and practical ActivityWatch productivity insights.

If you are tired of guessing which tasks, apps, or meetings consume your day, **ActivityWatch** is for you.
